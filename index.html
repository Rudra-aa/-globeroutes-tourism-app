Features

Built for True Wanderers

Every feature is designed to help you discover the world in full depth — from UNESCO wonders to that one waterfall nobody knows about.

5-Tier Fame Ranking

Attractions are color-graded from globally iconic red-tier wonders down to blue-tier hidden local secrets — see at a glance how popular a spot is.

City-by-City Travel Mode

Hop through cities in sequence within a country. Each hop reveals new tourism spots, regional cuisine tips, and local transit guides.

Infinite City Synthesis

Search any city on Earth — even tiny towns. The engine procedurally generates realistic POIs with coordinates, descriptions, and ratings.

Personal Travel Journal

Check in at landmarks you've visited and build your travel timeline. Earn pathfinder badges and track your world exploration progress.

Global Smart Search

Instantly search across all countries, cities, and attractions with autocomplete. Filter by tier, category, and entry cost on the fly.

Live Interactive Map

Color-coded markers on a real Leaflet map show all attractions in a city. Click any marker to get full attraction details, hours & tips.

About GlobeRoutes

What Is GlobeRoutes?

GlobeRoutes is a comprehensive world tourism mapping platform built for people who want to explore everything — not just what shows up on the first Google search.

Our Mission

To map every single tourism-worthy location on Earth — from the Eiffel Tower to an unnamed waterfall in a village no one has heard of.

Complete Coverage

We cover all 195 countries, every major and minor city, and use procedural AI to synthesize realistic POIs for locations with limited data.

Built for Developers & Travelers

GlobeRoutes is built with a developer-friendly architecture. Admin access, tiered content, and modular data make it easy to extend and customize.

Real-Time Generation

Search any city — even tiny obscure towns — and the platform generates realistic tourism spots, map coordinates, entry fees, and descriptions instantly.
